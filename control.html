Digital PID (P3): u[k] = Kp·e[k] + Ki·Ts·Σe[j] + Kd·(e[k] − e[k−1]) / Ts
Validation (K = 1, τ = 1 s): P only with Kp = 9 gives τ = 0.1 s and 10 % steady-state error. PI with Kp = Ki = 1 gives ωn = 1 rad/s and ζ = 1. Any PI gives zero steady-state error for a step.
