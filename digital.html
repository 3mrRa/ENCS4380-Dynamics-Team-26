Sampling

Log scale: 1 ms to 2 s. The controller only acts at t = 0, Ts, 2Ts, … and holds u constant in between (zero-order hold).

Anti-windup: while u is stuck at 0 % or 100 % and the error would push it further, the error sum stops growing.

PID gains

Digital PID: u[k] = Kp·e[k] + Ki·Ts·Σe[j] + Kd·(e[k] − e[k−1])/Ts
Error: e[k] = r − y(k·Ts)
Actuator: u = clamp(u[k], 0, 100 %), held until the next sample

Σe[j] is the sum of every error so far: times Ts it is the area under e(t) built from rectangles, the digital integral. (e[k] − e[k−1])/Ts is the slope between two samples, the digital derivative.

Plant (first order, from Module 1)

Plant: τ·dy/dt + y = K·u  ·  G(s) = K / (τs + 1)

u is in % (0–100). The highest output the plant can reach is K·100, so a setpoint near that keeps the actuator saturated for a long time.
