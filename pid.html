Plant model

The plant input is the actuator command u (0–100%). Plant gain K maps this command to the output scale.

PID controller

PID: u* = Kp·e + Ki·∫e dt − Kd·dy/dt
Error: e = r − y
Actuator: u = clamp(u*, 0, 100%)

Derivative is applied to the measured output to avoid a derivative kick when the setpoint changes. Integral accumulation is paused when saturation would drive the actuator further into its limit.

Numerical model

The differential equations are integrated using the forward Euler method with a 0.01 s time step. Change the plant and PID gains to see how the closed-loop response changes.
