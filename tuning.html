How it is simulated

The closed loop is stepped in time with RK4. Each plotted point is split into smaller steps when the gains make the loop fast. The integral stops growing while u is at a limit and the error pushes it further (anti-windup). The derivative acts on y, not on e, so the setpoint step gives no derivative kick.
