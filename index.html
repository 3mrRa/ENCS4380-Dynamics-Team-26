The model, from the lecture

τẏ + y = Kx,  so  G(s) = K / (τs + 1)
Step x = A:  y = KA(1 − e−t/τ),  63.2 % at τ, 95 % at 3τ
Ramp x = At:  y = KA(t − τ + τe−t/τ),  lag τ, steady error KAτ
Error fraction:  1 − y/KA = e−t/τ,  so  ln(error) = −t/τ
